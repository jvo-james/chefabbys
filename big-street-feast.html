Big Street Feast · Why it matters

A meal can do more than fill a plate.

Big Street Feast uses food as a practical way to bring attention to food access and community care. The initiative has grown through editions and partnerships, bringing people together in Accra and Kumasi around food, dignity and community care.

Behind every serving line are the cooks, volunteers and partners who make the work possible. Each edition is a reminder that generosity grows when a community pulls together.
