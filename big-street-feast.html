Big Street Feast is a community food initiative bringing people together around a simple belief: food should be a source of connection, not a privilege.

Get involved ↘
Why it matters

A meal can do more than fill a plate.

Big Street Feast uses food as a practical way to bring attention to food access and community care. The initiative has grown through editions and partnerships, with the aim of reaching people in Accra and Kumasi.

For the live website, this section can tell the story in Chef Abbys' own words, introduce the organisers and explain where contributions go.
