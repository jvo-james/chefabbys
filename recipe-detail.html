Ghana / Rice & grains

Jollof rice,
the conversation starter.

A familiar pot, a hundred opinions and plenty of stories. Jollof brings people to the table and keeps the conversation going.

Prep 20 min* Cook 45 min* Serves 4
A little context

Every pot has its own rhythm.

Before you begin

Jollof is a dish of layers. Start with a well-cooked pepper base, give the rice time to absorb the flavour and let the pot rest before serving.

From the kitchen

  1. Blend tomatoes, red peppers, onion and chilli into a smooth base. Prepare the aromatics and rinse the rice.
  2. Cook the onion in oil, add tomato paste then stir in the blended pepper mixture. Season and let the sauce reduce until rich.
  3. Add the rice and stock, stir to coat each grain then cover and cook gently until the rice is tender.
  4. Rest the pot for a few minutes before fluffing the rice. Serve with your favourite sides and a little extra pepper sauce.
Jollof is built in layers. The base, the rice and the gentle work of letting everything come together all matter.
