About Chef Abbys School of Food

Skills in the kitchen.
Stories beyond it.

Chef Abbys School of Food (CASOF) is the learning space within Chef Abbys World. It brings practical cooking, food heritage and the joy of learning around one table.

Explore learning paths built around African cooking, kitchen confidence and the stories behind the food.
