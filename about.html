A LITTLE ABOUT ME / 01
The journey so far

From sharing what was cooking to telling stories through food.

Chef Abbys, whose name is Abena Amoakoaa Sintim-Aboagye, began sharing her cooking online and grew that curiosity into a wider journey around African food, people and place.

Her work brings together cooking, travel and cultural storytelling. The point is never only what's on the plate. It's also the memory, the community and the hands that brought it there.

She describes herself as an African Food Tourist, a title that captures the curiosity at the heart of the work.
