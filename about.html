The woman behind the stories

Meet Chef Abbys.

Ghanaian chef, African Food Tourist and storyteller. She uses the kitchen to bring people closer to the places, memories and traditions behind the food.

Her story
The journey so far

From sharing what was cooking to telling stories through food.

Chef Abbys, whose name is Abena Amoakoaa Sintim-Aboagye, began sharing her cooking online and grew that curiosity into a wider journey around African food, people and place.

Her work brings together cooking, travel and cultural storytelling. The point is never only what is on the plate. It is also the memory, the community and the hands that brought it there.

Her first cooking videos were shared from home on Snapchat. A simple post about food opened a door, and she kept showing up. One of her early videos drew around 500 views, a small beginning that encouraged her to carry on.

Before pursuing food full-time, she studied Construction Management and worked in the engineering field. In interviews, she has spoken about choosing a path that felt like her own, even when a more predictable route was available.

Today, she describes herself as an African Food Tourist. Her videos move from familiar Ghanaian plates to dishes from other African countries, making room for the people, ingredients and stories behind each one. TIME described her work as part of a new generation of food creators shaping what audiences discover online.

What guides the work

Curiosity first.
Respect always.

Curiosity, respect, connection and community sit at the heart of the way she approaches food. They shape what she cooks, the people she meets and the stories she chooses to share.

Curiosity

Ask what's behind the dish.

Every ingredient and cooking method can open a door into history, place and everyday life.

Heritage

Make room for the people.

Give credit to the cooks, families, vendors and communities who keep food traditions alive.

Connection

Let the journey bring us closer.

Food is a common language, even when the ingredients and customs change from place to place.

Community

Let good work reach beyond the kitchen.

From food education to Big Street Feast, her work makes space for opportunity, generosity and practical impact.

From Ghana to new tables

When the kitchen travels.

In May 2026, Chef Abbys took Ghanaian food to Snapchat's London office. More than 300 employees got to try dishes prepared from her recipes alongside the in-house culinary team.

The UK visit also included cooking experiences and cultural collaborations, including a session with Ghana's High Commissioner in London. It was another chance to put Ghanaian flavours in front of new audiences.

More from the Journal
A voice for food creators

Recipes can travel. Ideas travel further.

Her work has moved beyond recipe videos into conversations about digital influence, food entrepreneurship and how African creators can build brands without losing their roots. In 2025, she represented Ghana at TikTok's showcase during the Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity. TikTok later described her as one of six African creators selected for its 2025 Global Discover List, and she was among 20 global creators invited to Cannes Lions.

In September 2026, she spoke with Berla Mundi on the B.You Podcast about choosing her own path. In November, she took that conversation to Social Fest 2026 in McAllen, Texas, where she spoke about how food creators turn recipes into reach.

She has also shared the story of meeting TikTok CEO Shou Zi Chew, a moment that brought home how far a journey that began with a simple cooking video had travelled. In November 2025, she was also recognised at the Pulse Influencer Awards, taking home honours across food, TikTok and innovation categories.
