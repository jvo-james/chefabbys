News / Food tour / August 2026

Six countries.
A whole lot of flavour.

A West Africa Food Tour was announced in August 2026, putting food, culture and travel at the centre of a new journey across the region.

CHEF ABBYS WORLD JOURNAL 5 MIN READ

The announced itinerary named six countries: Nigeria, Liberia, Togo, Senegal, Cabo Verde and Côte d’Ivoire. The tour was framed around the stories, food cultures and people that make each place distinct.

For Chef Abbys, food is a way into a bigger conversation. A traditional dish carries more than a recipe. It can hold family memory, local ingredients, regional history and the everyday choices of the people who cook it.

A journey told through the table

Each stop offers a chance to look beyond the familiar and meet the people who keep food traditions alive. A market visit, a conversation with a home cook or a meal shared around a table can tell a story that a list of ingredients cannot.

“When you cook a country's traditional food, you learn its values, its struggles, and its soul.” CHEF ABBYS · TIME100 CREATORS 2025 PROFILE
