Chef Abbys works at the meeting point of food, culture, travel and storytelling. This page helps brands and organisers understand possible ways to collaborate and gives the team a clear route to follow up.

01 / Brand partnerships

Put good stories on the table.

Campaigns, sponsored content, product storytelling and brand experiences that make sense for both sides.

02 / Events & appearances

Bring the food story to the room.

Speaking, hosting, cooking demonstrations, festival appearances and curated experiences.

03 / Tourism & culture

Show a place through its food.

Destination storytelling, culinary tourism, cultural campaigns and food-led travel collaborations.

04 / Press & media

Let's get the story right.

Interviews, press features, editorial requests and media appearances.

05 / Culinary education

Teach, taste and share.

Workshops, masterclasses and learning experiences connected to food culture.

06 / Community impact

Put purpose into practice.

Partnerships that support food access, community care and meaningful impact through initiatives such as Big Street Feast.

For media and partners

A little context before we meet.

The media kit should include an approved biography, brand story, selected work, confirmed audience statistics, previous collaborations, speaking topics and high-resolution photographs.

Audience reach, engagement figures and rates are intentionally not invented in this concept. The management team can supply verified information for a downloadable kit.
