What this course could cover

Learn the why as well as the how.

A course detail page can show learning outcomes, who the programme is for, course format, requirements and any materials students need. The outline below is proposed content and must be replaced by CASOF's approved curriculum.

01

Ingredients and the pantry

Get to know grains, spices, produce and foundational ingredients used across African kitchens.

02

Preparation and technique

Build practical confidence with preparation methods, kitchen organisation and flavour-building.

03

Food, culture and sharing

Explore the stories and traditions that give dishes meaning beyond their ingredients.
